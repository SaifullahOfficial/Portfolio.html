02 — Major AI Proposal

Smart AI Surveillance Engine

Real-Time Anomaly & Violence Detection via Autonomous Behavioral Video Analytics for Public Safety & Legacy CCTV Networks.

Autonomous Threat & Violence Sentinel

Islamia College Peshawar • Supervised by Dr. Muhammad Sajjad
Python 3.11 YOLOv8-Nano PyTorch OpenCV

Traditional CCTV networks suffer from passive monitoring blindspots: research confirms operators miss up to 95% of screen activity after 22 minutes. Our software-defined AI engine upgrades existing legacy cameras without costly hardware replacement, providing sub-500ms zero-latency threat detection for brawls, physical assault, and accidents.

Stage 01
Stream Ingestion
Decodes 25–30 FPS RTSP feeds via OpenCV with 16-frame sliding sequence batches.
Stage 02
Spatial Detection
YOLOv8-Nano isolates human bounding boxes (ROI) with sub-15ms localization speed.
Stage 03
Temporal Modeling
Dense Optical Flow + ConvLSTM network tracking kinetic acceleration spikes.
Stage 04
Inference & Alert
Softmax risk score trigger (>80%) activates Green/Red HUD bounding boxes & siren.
Global Benchmarks: RWF-2000 (2,000 CCTV Clips) UCF-Crime Dataset Custom Campus Hallway Set
