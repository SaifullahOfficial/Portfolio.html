01 — About Me

Who I Am

I'm Saifullah, a passionate web developer and entrepreneur based in Peshawar, Pakistan. With a strong eye for design and a love for clean, functional code, I create digital experiences that stand out.

I'm a proud member of NIC Peshawar (National Incubation Center), where our 3–4 member team co-developed joyframes.app — an innovative application built at NIC Peshawar.

Beyond development, I'm also the Founder & Owner of Wear Well Collection (wearwellcollection.com), a fashion brand where I apply my digital skills to drive e-commerce and brand growth online.

I specialize in crafting UI/UX designs with Figma and turning those designs into reality using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React.
