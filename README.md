### **Smart AI Surveillance: Real-Time Anomaly & Violence Detection**
*Autonomous Behavioral Video Analytics for Public Safety & Legacy CCTV Networks*  
**Course Supervisor:** Dr. Muhammad Sajjad (Associate Professor, Islamia College Peshawar)

```
[RTSP CCTV Stream] ──> [OpenCV 25-30 FPS Ingestion] ──> [YOLOv8-Nano ROI Localization]
                                                                  │
[Zero-Latency Alerts] <── [Softmax Risk Trigger (>80%)] <── [ConvLSTM Temporal Kinetic Modeling]
```

- 📊 **Global Benchmarks:** Evaluated on **RWF-2000** (Real-World Fight Dataset) and **UCF-Crime** datasets.
