The Artist Behind the Canvas

"Art is how we tether fleeting emotions to a permanent canvas."

Based in the United Kingdom, Mishi creates delicate, atmospheric fine art combining traditional watercolor, heavy gouache, oils, and genuine gold leaf. Her work draws inspiration from cultural architecture, introspective emotional landscapes, and the untamed botanicals of the English countryside.

Every original painting and limited archival print is treated as a lasting heirloom. From hand-torn deckled edges to museum-grade archival pigments, each piece is crafted to bring stillness, warmth, and enduring beauty into your living space.

Studio Preservation Guide

How to Care for Your Art

1

Framing & Glazing

For fine art cotton rag prints and watercolor originals, always use acid-free mounting tape and archival matting. We recommend UV-filtering Artglass to preserve pigment brilliance for generations.

2

Placement & Lighting

Avoid placing works in direct prolonged sunlight, above active radiators, or in high-humidity areas like unventilated bathrooms.

3

Handling Deckled Edges

When handling original deckled-edge paper or silk tassel bookmarks, hold them by the edges using clean, dry hands to protect the tactile fibers.
